About Our Club

Culture Travel Club is a fictional travel club that helps curious people discover the history and everyday life of cities. We believe that understanding local culture makes travel more meaningful.

Travel Tip

Bring comfortable shoes and leave time to explore on foot.

Who can join the club?

Anyone interested in history, culture, and discovering new places is welcome.
